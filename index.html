Our Story

Born from Soil,
Grown with Passion

La Fleur was born in the lush green campus of the College of Agriculture, Vellanikkara — a group of BSc Agriculture students who turned their passion for floriculture into something beautiful and meaningful.

Every plant we nurture, every terrarium we craft, every piece of resin art we pour — is made with the knowledge from our classrooms and the love we have for the natural world. We believe nature's beauty deserves to live in your home.

"We are not just selling plants. We are sharing a piece of Kerala's lush botanical soul, handcrafted by the next generation of agriculture scientists." — La Fleur Founding Team, KAU Vellanikkara

Terrariums — A World in Glass

Imagine a tiny forest, a desert oasis, or a lush green world — all contained in a beautiful glass vessel sitting on your desk. Our terrariums are living sculptures that grow and evolve with you.

Crafted with knowledge from our Horticulture and Plant Science courses, each terrarium is built for longevity and beauty. We source the best mosses, ferns, pebbles and activated charcoal to create the perfect micro-ecosystem.

How We Work

From Campus to Your Doorstep

01
Grow & Source

We grow and curate plants, flowers and materials directly on our college campus and trusted local farms in Kerala.

02
Handcraft

Every product is made by hand — from arranging terrariums to pouring resin art pieces — with care and precision.

03
Quality Check

Each piece is inspected using our horticultural knowledge to ensure it's healthy, beautiful and ready for you.

04
Delivered to You

Safely packaged and delivered across Kerala. Local pickup available at our campus in Vellanikkara, Thrissur.
